# Candidate screening and fixed breakthrough threshold

**Screening date:** 2026-08-08  
**Threshold fixed before the final search:** exact determination of at least one source-located unresolved Ramsey value, with both bounds independently auditable. A one-sided bound, a larger computational search that leaves an interval, or another example consistent with an existing theorem would not qualify.

## Operational definitions

- **Novel result:** not located in prior literature, but potentially routine or low-impact.
- **Incremental advance:** modest bound improvement, additional witness, replication, or parameter extension that leaves the core question unresolved.
- **Minor breakthrough:** decisive frontier change in a narrow line, such as closing a previously open exact value or overturning a substantive conjecture.
- **Major breakthrough:** a broad theorem, infinite family, or field-wide conceptual change.

The minimum acceptable outcome was fixed as a **minor breakthrough**. It was not lowered after results were observed.

## Ordinal candidate ranking

Scores are 1 (low) to 5 (high). “Novelty confidence” means confidence that a successful result would not duplicate indexed prior work. The composite is the sum, used only as a triage aid rather than a probabilistic estimate.

The two adjacent Ramsey targets were pursued first. The `n=259` upper side yielded an exact certificate, but a matching lower construction was not obtained, so it remained below the fixed threshold. The `n=260` target produced both a 276-vertex extremal witness and an exact upper proof, and therefore became the completed study.
